Account Deletion & Data Removal

Instructions for deleting your Renewal Desk account and associated gym data

Permanent Deletion Warning
Deleting your account is permanent. All your gym records, member directory, membership plans, message history, and payment logs will be deleted from our active databases.
Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

You can instantly delete your account directly inside the Renewal Desk Android mobile app:

  1. Open the Renewal Desk app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the More / Settings icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account Management section.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Data.
  5. Confirm the deletion prompt. Your account and gym data will be immediately removed and you will be signed out.
Method 2: Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, submit a deletion request by email:

  • Send an email to support@revorax.online from your registered account email.
  • Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request - Renewal Desk
  • Include your registered gym name and email address.

Email deletion requests are processed within 48 hours of verification.

What Data Is Deleted?
  • Account Profile: Owner/staff names, email addresses, password hashes, and session tokens.
  • Gym Records: Gym profile, address, operating hours, and configuration.
  • Member Database: All member contact details, attendance, and expiry dates.
  • Payment & Messages: Manual payment records and WhatsApp conversation logs.

Aggregated transactional financial logs required by applicable tax or commercial accounting regulations may be retained for the minimum statutory period.

Questions about your data or privacy? Contact our Data Protection Team at support@revorax.online.
