Privacy Policy

Renewal Desk (Gym Management) & VYNLA (Member Mobile Experience)

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Renewal Desk and VYNLA ("we", "our", or "us"), operated by Revorax, provide gym management software, automated membership renewal reminders, member self-service mobile applications, and access control integrations. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile applications:

  • Renewal Desk: (Android package: online.revorax.renewaldesk) — Staff & Gym Management application.
  • VYNLA: (Android package: online.revorax.vynla) — Consumer & Gym Member application.

By using Renewal Desk or VYNLA, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

For Gym Owners and Staff:

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, phone number, and password hash.
  • Gym Profile: Gym name, business phone number, operating address, timezone, and payment details (such as UPI ID or payment instructions).

For Gym Members:

  • Contact Information: Full name, phone number, and optional email address.
  • Membership Records: Plan name, duration, start and expiry dates, payment claim records, and verification status.
  • Attendance Data: Timestamped gate entry and exit event logs captured via access control integrations.

Technical & Device Information:

  • Push notification device tokens (Expo Notifications) to deliver renewal alerts and verification notices.
  • Application crash reports and diagnostic performance logs (Sentry) to maintain stability.
3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Providing core gym administration, member tracking, and plan management.
  • Sending automated WhatsApp renewal reminders and payment receipts on behalf of your gym.
  • Enabling member self-service logins via secure WhatsApp OTP verification.
  • Processing manual and self-service payment verification requests.
  • Managing gym turnstile and biometric access control states.
  • Delivering push notifications regarding membership renewals and urgent updates.
4. Third-Party Service Providers

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform:

  • Meta WhatsApp Cloud API: Used to dispatch transactional WhatsApp notifications and OTP verification codes.
  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution, Google Sign-In authentication, and Google Play In-App Purchases.
  • Expo / Push Notification Services: Used to transmit push notifications to registered devices.
  • Sentry: Used for real-time error reporting and stability monitoring.

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal or member data to any third party or advertisers.

5. Data Security & Storage

All communications between mobile applications, desktop bridge software, and backend servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Authentication tokens are securely hashed and stored on devices using encrypted storage (Android Keystore / SecureStore). Strict multi-tenant isolation ensures each gym's data is partitioned and protected against unauthorized access.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Users have the right to request deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

  • In-App Deletion: Owners can navigate to More → Account Management → Delete Account & Data. Members can initiate deletion from Profile → Delete Account.
  • Web Deletion Portal: You can visit our public deletion page at https://gym-production-910c.up.railway.app/delete-account.
  • Email Request: Submit a deletion request to support@revorax.online.

Upon verified request, user accounts and associated records are permanently erased from active databases.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental or guardian consent.
